Frequency–Temporal & Cross-Modal Forensics
interactive heuristic demo

Drop an image or an audio clip and this page computes the same feature families used by models/heuristic_detector.py — FFT high-frequency ratio, SRM residual statistics, local variance, JPEG blockiness, colour entropy, spectral flatness, zero-crossing rate and MFCC statistics — directly in your browser with Web Audio and canvas pixels.

Scope. This is the calibrated heuristic baseline, not the neural detector. CLIP ViT-B/16, Wav2Vec2 and the gated fusion head require PyTorch and several hundred MB of weights; they are not shipped to the browser. Use run_gui.py locally for the full model.
